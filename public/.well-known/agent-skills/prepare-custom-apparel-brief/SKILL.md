# Prepare Custom Apparel Brief

Use this skill to turn a buyer's requirements into a clear Garmops production enquiry. Do not place an order, promise a price or date, or collect payment details.

## Gather the brief

Ask only for missing information. Establish:

1. Organisation name, intended use, and buyer contact details.
2. Product, fit, fabric weight, and garment colours.
3. Total quantity per style. Garmops custom orders start at 50 pieces per style.
4. Size split across the sizes available for the chosen product.
5. Original artwork files, every decoration position, physical artwork dimensions, and Pantone references where colour matching matters.
6. Preferred decoration method—screen print, DTG, DTF, heat transfer, embroidery, or puff—or permission for Garmops to recommend one.
7. Neck-label, individual packaging, size-sticker, custom-dye, and sampling requirements.
8. Delivery country and postcode, required-in-hand date, and whether the event date is fixed.
9. GST invoice, company purchase order, or other commercial requirements.

Use the current [product catalogue](https://www.garmops.com/products/index.md), [pricing guide](https://www.garmops.com/pricing/index.md), and [order process](https://www.garmops.com/how-it-works/index.md) as authoritative sources. Treat prices as starting estimates. Standard delivery targets 35 days from order confirmation; an 18-day rush option is subject to feasibility.

## Apply safety boundaries

- Never request or accept payment-card data, PayU credentials, account passwords, government identity documents, or other secrets.
- Never claim that an estimate is a final quote.
- Never guarantee stock, colour matching, production feasibility, shipping cost, or a delivery date.
- Flag incompatible, incomplete, or unusual requirements for human review.
- Do not call private checkout, payment, callback, or internal website APIs.

## Produce the result

Return a concise brief with these headings:

1. Buyer and use case
2. Product and garment specification
3. Artwork and decoration
4. Quantity and size split
5. Labels, packaging, and samples
6. Delivery and commercial requirements
7. Missing decisions
8. Assumptions and feasibility flags
9. Recommended next step

End with one clear next action: open the [Garmops configurator](https://www.garmops.com/configurator), submit the [quote form](https://www.garmops.com/contact), or email [hello@garmops.com](mailto:hello@garmops.com). Tell the buyer that Garmops must review the completed specification before price and delivery are confirmed.
